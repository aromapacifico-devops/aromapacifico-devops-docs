# Assumptions

Everything the design takes as true without a source in the pilot definition. Each row states what would change if the assumption failed.

| Assumption | Basis | What changes if it fails | Date | Author | Issue |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| The farm node reaches the zones of cultivation and drying with one enclosure and wired probes | Minimum fixed hardware on the farm | A second small node per farm, with higher hardware cost | 2026-11-05 | Melo088 | [#23](https://github.com/aromapacifico-devops/aromapacifico-devops-docs/issues/23) |
| A shared LoRaWAN gateway can reach the out-of-coverage farms of a vereda from one high point with cellular signal | No new base station is allowed | Those farms need another link, such as satellite, or a data mule for readings as well | 2026-11-05 | Melo088 | [#18](https://github.com/aromapacifico-devops/aromapacifico-devops-docs/issues/18) |
| LTE is available on the routes and at the high points that matter | Current LTE-M and NB-IoT coverage in Colombia is unconfirmed | The node modem and the gateway backhaul change | 2026-11-05 | Melo088 | [#22](https://github.com/aromapacifico-devops/aromapacifico-devops-docs/issues/22) |
| Readings older than 24 hours in a full buffer are discarded and counted | The local buffer retains 24 hours at most | Storage on the node and the loss report change | 2026-11-05 | Melo088 | [#25](https://github.com/aromapacifico-devops/aromapacifico-devops-docs/issues/25) |
| Drone objects upload over HTTPS from the municipal seat or the collection center | Missions are preloaded at those same places | The object path and its subnet change | 2026-11-05 | Melo088 | [#36](https://github.com/aromapacifico-devops/aromapacifico-devops-docs/issues/36) |
| Harvest lasts four months a year | Team estimate for cost purposes | Monthly cost figures change | 2026-11-05 | Melo088 | [#51](https://github.com/aromapacifico-devops/aromapacifico-devops-docs/issues/51) |
| Three drone brigades, one per department, produce about 9,000 images in a harvest month | Team estimate for cost purposes | Inference and storage costs change | 2026-11-05 | Melo088 | [#54](https://github.com/aromapacifico-devops/aromapacifico-devops-docs/issues/54) |
| A designated drone-service supervisor authorizes fumigation, distinct from the pilot who flies | Human authorization with MFA is required | The authorizer role moves to another identity | 2026-11-05 | Melo088 | [#30](https://github.com/aromapacifico-devops/aromapacifico-devops-docs/issues/30) |
| Alerts reach the farmer by mobile push, and an SMS fallback stays optional | The price for Colombia could not be read from the price list | Alerts wait until the phone has data | 2026-11-05 | Melo088 | [#26](https://github.com/aromapacifico-devops/aromapacifico-devops-docs/issues/26) |
| The panel gives at least 3 peak sun hours on an average day | Needs field data | A larger panel or battery | 2026-11-05 | Melo088 | [#24](https://github.com/aromapacifico-devops/aromapacifico-devops-docs/issues/24) |
| Images average 3 MB | Common for a drone photo, to confirm with the supplier | Storage cost scales linearly | 2026-11-05 | Melo088 | [#54](https://github.com/aromapacifico-devops/aromapacifico-devops-docs/issues/54) |
| The demonstration runs on standalone AWS accounts with promotional credits | The team has those accounts, and joining an Organization would move them to the paid plan | One account with name prefixes, or a paid account with a budget | 2026-11-06 | Melo088 | [#4](https://github.com/aromapacifico-devops/aromapacifico-devops-docs/issues/4) |
| IoT Core, Bedrock and VPN are usable on a free plan account | AWS lists only select services and names no list for these | Those variables stay off in the demonstration, and the plan still proves the code | 2026-11-06 | Melo088 | [#4](https://github.com/aromapacifico-devops/aromapacifico-devops-docs/issues/4) |
| Three of the seven subnets hold interface endpoints and four stay reserved | Managed services and functions run outside subnets | A data or query service moves into a subnet | 2026-11-06 | Melo088 | [#12](https://github.com/aromapacifico-devops/aromapacifico-devops-docs/issues/12) |
| The United States is a valid destination for personal data under Colombian rules, with contractual clauses if needed | The status on the regulator's list is unconfirmed | Contract terms change, or personal data moves to another region | 2026-11-05 | Melo088 | [#9](https://github.com/aromapacifico-devops/aromapacifico-devops-docs/issues/9) |
| The five years of personal data start when the relationship ends | The pilot gives the term and no starting point | Data of active farmers and carriers needs another legal basis to stay past five years | 2026-11-06 | Melo088 | [#9](https://github.com/aromapacifico-devops/aromapacifico-devops-docs/issues/9) |
| A node connection averages 1.5 W, 45 seconds with signal and 120 seconds at most without | Estimate for a cellular modem, to measure with the chosen one | The share of the energy budget scales linearly, from 4 percent at the worst case | 2026-11-06 | Melo088 | [#17](https://github.com/aromapacifico-devops/aromapacifico-devops-docs/issues/17) |
| One connection costs about 6 KB in TLS and MQTT overhead | A mutual TLS handshake exchanges certificates, to measure on the first nodes | The monthly data plan of a node scales linearly | 2026-11-06 | Melo088 | [#17](https://github.com/aromapacifico-devops/aromapacifico-devops-docs/issues/17) |
| The carrier gives network time, or UDP port 123 is reachable | TLS needs a valid clock before the connection | The node needs another time source | 2026-11-06 | Melo088 | [#17](https://github.com/aromapacifico-devops/aromapacifico-devops-docs/issues/17) |
| The node has a real-time clock on backup power | Readings need a time during sleep and after a brownout | Readings wait for a correction from elapsed time, and a full power loss costs their time | 2026-11-06 | Melo088 | [#17](https://github.com/aromapacifico-devops/aromapacifico-devops-docs/issues/17) |
| Carriers use Android or iOS phones and accept location at all times during a trip | A smartphone with data is part of the pilot, and a 5-minute interval needs background location | A dedicated device, or a lower expectation for the route | 2026-11-06 | Melo088 | [#20](https://github.com/aromapacifico-devops/aromapacifico-devops-docs/issues/20) |
| The phone stays charged in the vehicle during a trip | A location service running for 10 hours drains a phone with no charger | Battery becomes a limit, and the interval may need review | 2026-11-06 | Melo088 | [#20](https://github.com/aromapacifico-devops/aromapacifico-devops-docs/issues/20) |
| A sack rejected at a center physically returns to its farm | Rejected coffee stays the property of the farmer, and the pilot names no other destination | The return event changes, and a new code per attempt may fit better | 2026-11-06 | Melo088 | [#42](https://github.com/aromapacifico-devops/aromapacifico-devops-docs/issues/42) |
| The carrier can record the delivery at the port, with the receiver and its receipt | No Federation staff or equipment exists at the port | The port needs another role or a record from a third party | 2026-11-06 | Melo088 | [#42](https://github.com/aromapacifico-devops/aromapacifico-devops-docs/issues/42) |
| Pre-printed labels can reach farms with people already traveling, such as carriers and field staff | Those people already visit the farms | Label logistics needs its own cost and route | 2026-11-06 | Melo088 | [#43](https://github.com/aromapacifico-devops/aromapacifico-devops-docs/issues/43) |
| The receiver at the port issues a receipt with a reference the carrier can enter | A load changing hands normally leaves a document | The evidence falls to the scan and the location | 2026-11-06 | Melo088 | [#21](https://github.com/aromapacifico-devops/aromapacifico-devops-docs/issues/21) |
