# The drone confirms a circle around the node that raised the alert

## Context

A cultivation reading out of range opens a case, and the drone must confirm the symptom on the place the reading marked without sweeping the farm. The node is installed among the trees it measures, and its position was not recorded anywhere.

## Options

A focus built from the recorded position of the node, or a focus that a person must locate before any mission.

## Decision

The installer records the lot and the GPS position of each node. The focus is a circle of 50 m around that position, cut to the published lot polygon and outside no-fly zones, and a supervisor can move it. A symptom reported by a farmer uses the position of the phone. Only cultivation alerts and reported symptoms open a case, with one open case per node, and drying alerts only notify the farmer.

## Consequences

A case requests its confirmation with no human step, and the flight covers a small area of one lot. A node with no recorded position leaves its cases waiting for the supervisor. The radius is a parameter to check with the first cases.
