# The cost model assumes three brigades and four months of harvest

## Context

The pilot figures leave out the fleet, the number of photos and the harvest length, and costs depend on them.

## Options

Estimate them, or leave costs as ranges.

## Decision

The model assumes four harvest months a year, one drone brigade per department and about 9,000 images in a harvest month.

## Consequences

Costs stay traceable to stated inputs, which the cost document exposes as editable values.
