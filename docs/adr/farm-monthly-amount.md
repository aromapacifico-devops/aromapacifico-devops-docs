# The monthly amount of a farm recovers its recurring cost with no margin

## Context

A farmer must judge whether the investment and the monthly amount are moderate. The Federation has set no fee policy, so no fee can be stated. The [farm hardware and fee](../governance/farm-hardware-and-fee.md) estimate computes the investment, the cash cost, the replacement reserve and the fixed cloud lines for 200 to 1,000 farms, with essential parts still to quote.

## Options

A recovers only the marginal cash cost. B adds the cash cost of the gateways and the replacement reserve, and the Federation bears the fixed cloud lines. C adds the fixed cloud lines divided by the farms.

## Decision

Option C, as a provisional proposal of the team. It recovers everything recurring with no margin and no financing, and the initial investment is presented apart. In the base scenario its known part is USD 11.77 a farm a month at 200 farms and USD 11.07 at 1,000.

## Consequences

- The monthly amount covers operation and the replacements, so the platform does not need a second charge for worn parts.
- The fixed cloud lines are assigned in full to the farms, although carriers, centers and buyers also use the platform, so the amount is higher than a shared assignment would give.
- The amount rises when the pending parts are quoted, through their reserve. It is not a fee until the Federation approves a policy, which may add a subsidy, a margin or financing.
